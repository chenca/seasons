Seasons - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Seasons is a standalone screensaver for Roku. It displays a lone cherry tree on a hill through four seasons, day and night.

Information we collect

Seasons collects no personal information. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, or any account information. It does not use an account, sign-in, advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

Network access

Seasons does not require an internet connection and does not send or receive data over the network. All images and animation are included in the app package on your Roku device.

Children

Seasons collects no information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here, with the last updated date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to muse@chenca.org.
